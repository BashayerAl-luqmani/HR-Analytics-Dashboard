# HR Analytics Dashboard - Employee Attrition Analysis

## Project Overview

The HR Analytics Dashboard is a data-driven Power BI project designed to analyze employee attrition patterns, workforce distribution, and the factors that influence employee turnover.

The dashboard transforms raw employee data into meaningful insights that help HR teams understand why employees leave and identify opportunities to improve employee retention.

---

# Business Problem

Employee turnover is a major challenge for organizations as it increases recruitment costs, affects workforce stability, and impacts overall productivity.

The HR department needs a better understanding of employee attrition patterns and the key factors associated with employee departures, such as department, salary level, job role, satisfaction, experience, and demographics.

This project analyzes employee data to answer important business questions and support data-driven HR decisions.

---

# Business Questions

The dashboard was developed to answer the following questions:

### 1. Which departments have the highest employee attrition?
Identify departments experiencing the highest number of employee departures to understand where retention efforts are needed.

### 2. Does salary level influence employee attrition?
Analyze whether employees in specific salary ranges are more likely to leave the company.

### 3. Which job roles are most affected by employee turnover?
Understand which positions experience higher attrition and how job satisfaction contributes to employee decisions.

### 4. Is employee satisfaction related to attrition?
Explore the relationship between job satisfaction levels and employee turnover patterns.

### 5. At what experience levels do employees leave the most?
Analyze whether attrition is concentrated among new employees or experienced staff.

### 6. What are the main workforce demographic patterns?
Understand employee distribution based on age groups and gender.

# Dashboard Visualizations

The dashboard contains 7 main visuals:

## 1. Attrition by Department

**Visual Type:** Donut Chart

**Purpose:**
Shows the distribution of employee attrition across different departments.

**Key Insight:**
Identifies departments with higher employee turnover rates.

---

## 2. Attrition by Salary Slab

**Visual Type:** Clustered Bar Chart

**Purpose:**
Compares employees who stayed and employees who left across different salary ranges.

**Key Insight:**
Helps analyze whether compensation levels are related to employee turnover.

---

## 3. Attrition by Job Role & Satisfaction

**Visual Type:** Matrix / Heatmap

**Purpose:**
Analyzes the relationship between job roles, satisfaction levels, and employee attrition.

**Key Insight:**
Highlights roles where low satisfaction may contribute to higher turnover.

---

## 4. Department-Wise Employee Count

**Visual Type:** Funnel Chart

**Purpose:**
Displays workforce size across different departments.

**Key Insight:**
Provides an overview of employee distribution within the organization.

---

## 5. Attrition Trend by Experience

**Visual Type:** Line Chart

**Purpose:**
Analyzes employee departures based on years of experience.

**Key Insight:**
Identifies experience stages where employees are more likely to leave.

---

## 6. Attrition by Gender

**Visual Type:** Pie Chart

**Purpose:**
Shows attrition distribution between male and female employees.

**Key Insight:**
Provides demographic insights into employee turnover.

---

## 7. Age Group Distribution

**Visual Type:** Column Chart

**Purpose:**
Shows the workforce structure based on employee age groups.

**Key Insight:**
Highlights the main age segments within the organization.
